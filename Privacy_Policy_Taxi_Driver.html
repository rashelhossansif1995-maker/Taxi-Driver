🔒 Privacy Policy for Taxi Driver

Thank you for using the Taxi Driver mobile application. Protecting the privacy of user information is one of our top priorities. This Privacy Policy explains the rules regarding the use of data by the app.

1. Location and Location Data

The app does not currently use or collect the precise location (GPS Location) of your device for its current features. Navigation and route-tracking features, as well as related data collection, may be introduced in the future.

Purpose of use: If location-based features are introduced in the future, location information may be used for taxi driver income and expense management, distance calculation, navigation assistance, and route tracking.

2. Data Collection & Local Storage

Personal Information: At present, the app does not collect or store confidential or sensitive personal data on external servers operated by the developer.

Local Storage: App settings and taxi-driver-related information entered into the app are stored only in the local memory of your device (Local Storage).

Data-collection servers or server-based features may be introduced in the future. If this happens, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the applicable data practices.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support free services, the app may display Google AdMob or other advertisements in the future.

Advertising-related Data: If Google AdMob is used, Google may use the device's Advertising ID, IP Address, and technical information to display relevant advertisements.

Controls: You may be able to turn off personalized advertising or manage/delete your Advertising ID through the advertising settings available on your device, such as Settings > Google > Ads, depending on your device and Android version.

4. Future Data Collection

The app developer does not currently operate a data-collection server for the app. If server-based data collection or additional online services are introduced in the future, the Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is not designed for children, and no data is knowingly collected from children.

6. Future Changes

The app's features, data collection practices, server services, navigation, route tracking, or advertising may change in the future. Any applicable changes will be reflected in an updated Privacy Policy.
